## How codex understood the assignment

Keep the island's damp, vertical geography and the urgent, muscular energy of a 1990s action thriller, but allow the casting to change the emotional temperature. Instead of hard-edged human commandos, the film has wet-nosed, round-eared chiguiros in rain-dark tactical gear, attempting very serious operations with paws that never quite suggest tactical competence.
