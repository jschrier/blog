## How Codex understood the Assignment

The 2023 post paired a film image with a capybara photograph. This time, I examined the **left-hand film image** in each collage and wrote a new image-generation instruction that translates its composition, costume, weather, and emotional tone into a capybara-cast period epic. The source image comes first, followed by the new instruction and the resulting still.

The aim is not a literal reproduction: it is a fresh, chiguiro-forward interpretation of what makes each source image memorable—blue winter night, fur collars, snow, antique timber, distance, and longing.
