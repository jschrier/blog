## How codex understood the assignment

This is a return trip to the earlier [Imaginary Cinema experiment]({{ site.baseurl }}{% post_url 2024-09-28-Imaginary-Cinema:-Top-Gun,-but-with-Chiguiros %}), now using a fresh storyboard and a direct image-generation pass. The goal was to retain the velocity, glamorous light, and sincere melodrama of a 1980s aviation movie, while letting capybara faces, ears, and thoroughly impractical paws determine the casting.

## Storyboard, before the chiguiros

1. At dawn, two daring young fighter pilots are thrown down an aircraft-carrier catapult as steam rolls over the deck and the ocean catches the first gold light.
2. A veteran squadron commander runs a tense mission brief beneath a projector beam, while elite trainees lean over a glowing tactical map.
3. Two cocky aviators leave a beachside officer club in dress whites, pause beside a motorcycle, and project a dangerous amount of confidence.
4. A lone fighter cuts through an impossibly blue sky, flips inverted above another jet, and turns a routine training hop into a personal argument at Mach speed.
5. The squadron spills onto the beach after flying, competing at volleyball in a haze of salt, sunscreen, and late-afternoon light.
6. A pilot flies into a moonless storm, the cockpit momentarily lit white by lightning and the ocean barely visible below.
7. In a locker room full of helmets and metal lockers, two rivals trade a silent look that says the next flight will settle everything.
8. An instructor and a young pilot share a quiet moment on a pier, with a parked motorcycle, wind-whipped water, and a sunset that is doing far too much.
9. During a dangerous overwater engagement, one fighter lures an opponent away while the wingman races in from the sun.
10. Back on the carrier at sunrise, the squadron gathers around a returning jet; victory is exhausted, salty, and briefly uncomplicated.
