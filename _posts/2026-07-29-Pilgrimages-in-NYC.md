Catholic pilgrimage sites in NYC...

- 10 Jan 2026-10 Jan 2027...[pilgrimages to any Franciscan Conventual Church have a plenary indulgence](https://myfranciscan.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Decreto_Indulgenza_Giubileo_Francescono-EN.pdf). (Usual [terms](https://www.vatican.va/content/catechism/en/part_two/section_two/chapter_two/article_4/x_indulgences.html) and [conditions](https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/tribunals/apost_penit/documents/rc_trib_appen_pro_20000129_indulgence_en.html) apply.)  [St. Adalbert](https://maps.app.goo.gl/F6HxEZrrB3ohD9S17) in Elmhurst, Queens, is to my knowledge the only one in NYC.

- Dorothy Day pilgrimage (an approximately 2 mile walking tour in the Village). The [Dorothy Day Guild offers this periodically, as well as providing a DIY guide](https://www.dorothydayguild.org/walking-tour) and [bloggers have written about it too](https://cracksinpomo.substack.com/p/the-dorothy-day-pilgrimage)

- In the 1930s, Monsignor Pasquale Lombardo built a replica of the Lourdes shrine in the East Bronx [St. Lucy's parish](https://stlucybronx.org). ([map](https://maps.app.goo.gl/6WCmKvXCs1uq6Kpd6)) and [bloggers have written about it too](https://citylore.org/taste-holy-water-from-our-lady-of-lourdes-grotto/); when my grandmother was downsizing her house, she had a collection of unused postcards from the 1940-1960s, a few of which were from this site.

- The Archdiocsese of NY has a [list of local shrines](https://archwaysmag.org/one-day-pilgrimages); St. Elizabeth Ann Seton & St. Frances Cabrini Shrines are in Manhattan, others are a bit further afield outside the city.
